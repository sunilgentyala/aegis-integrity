## Abstract

We describe a federated method for detecting anomalies on low-power edge devices and compare it with centralised baselines on simulated sensor traffic.

## 1. Introduction

Federated learning keeps raw data on the device [1]. Surveys of anomaly detection for sensor streams [2] and gradient compression [3] motivate the design. Communication cost has been studied extensively [4], [9], as have privacy attacks on shared updates [5], [6]. Practical deployments face unreliable links [7], [8], and benchmark choice strongly affects reported results [10], [11], [12].

## 2. Method

Each device trains a small autoencoder locally and shares only clipped model updates.
